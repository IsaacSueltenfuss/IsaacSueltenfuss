### American Football
* **[New York Giants Pass Protection Report](https://github.com/IsaacSueltenfuss/nfl-giants-pass-protection)**
  * Evaluates pass protection efficiency and pressure mitigation using NFL tracking and play-by-play data.
  * `Python` • `nflverse` • `Tableau` • `Data Visualization`
* **[New York Giants Passing Analysis](https://github.com/IsaacSueltenfuss/nfl-giants-passing-efficiency)**
  * Evaluates the evolution of the Giants offense, contextualized by injuries and aggregated passing metrics. Profiles Jaxson Dart’s tendencies and growth over the season.
  * `Python` • `nflverse` • `Tableau` • `Data Visualization`
* **[New York Jets Spatial Tracking Analysis](https://github.com/IsaacSueltenfuss/nfl-jets-spatial-tracking)**
  * Creates a play-by-play spatial tracking pipeline, which is then used to evaluate the success of the Jets defense and their ability to close distance and minimize passing windows.
  * `Python` • `NGS` • `Big Data Bowl` • `Data Visualization`
* **[NFL Draft Position Analysis](https://github.com/IsaacSueltenfuss/nfl-draft-position-analysis)**
  * Evaluates a team’s draft philosophy by observing weighted draft rounds and pick numbers for each position. This data is compiled for the NFC East teams and compared.
  * `Python` • `nflverse` • `Tableau` • `Data Visualization`
